Delete your AI Keyboard data

AI Keyboard does not use a sign-in account. The app gets an anonymous ID when you first use an AI feature. You delete the data for that ID from inside the app.

Delete your data in the app

  1. Open the AI Keyboard app.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Tap Delete my data and confirm.

Your phone must be online. The app deletes the data on our server first, and then the data on your phone.

What we delete

  • Your AI usage counters.
  • The link between your anonymous ID and your Pro subscription.
  • The device integrity check result.
  • AI output reports that you sent.
  • Your anonymous ID.

We delete this data at once. We do not keep a copy. Google Play keeps its own purchase records.

Cancel your subscription

Deleting your data does not cancel a Pro subscription. To cancel, open Google Play subscriptions, select AI Keyboard and tap Cancel subscription.

If you cannot use the app
